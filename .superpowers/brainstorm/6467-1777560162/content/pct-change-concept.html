Key behaviors

  • each bar shows the % change from the previous bar's close price
  • positive bars (green) extend upward from the zero line
  • negative bars (red) extend downward from the zero line
  • bar height is proportional to the magnitude of % change
  • same timeframe as the candlestick chart above it
  • color coding matches your trading signals (green=buy, red=sell)
